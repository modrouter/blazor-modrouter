# Known limitations

Every open question the slice reviews left behind, each decided once. Back to the [README](../README.md).

The seven per-slice follow-up documents listed 77 bolded items. Each appears below exactly once, grouped by
area, with one status:

- **fixed in 4d**: changed in slice 4d; the commit subject is named.
- **fixed in 4e**: changed in slice 4e; the commit subject and the tests that pin it are named.
- **fixed earlier**: an earlier slice fixed it; the slice and the code or test that shows it are named.
- **accept**: the router behaves this way on purpose or at an acceptable cost; the note gives the reason and what to expect.
- **fix later**: a known gap; the note gives the trigger that should reopen it.

The first column of each table is the item as the review put it, the second the slice whose review raised it (`1`, `2`, `3a`, `3b`,
`4a`, `4b`, `4c`). The totals: 13 items from slice 1, 16 from slice 2, 5 from 3a, 10 from 3b, 10 from 4a, 14 from 4b and 9 from 4c
(77 in the area tables), plus 6 notes in those documents that were not bolded ([unbolded notes](#notes-that-were-not-bolded)), 8

([found in slice 4e](#found-in-slice-4e)): 101 rows in all. Slice 4e took on the 15 rows that were **fix later**: 12 are fixed and 3 are now
